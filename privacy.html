Privacy

What this website stores

Your cart is saved in your own browser (localStorage) so it is still there when you come back. It is not sent to us and you can clear it any time by emptying your cart or clearing your browser data.

Payments

When you pay, you are sent to Stripe's secure page. Stripe handles your name, address and card details. We never see or store your card number.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use advertising cookies or tracking. If analytics are added later, this page must be updated.

Fonts

The fonts load from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google when a page is opened.
