About VANTA/9

VANTA/9 is streetwear for the night shift: heavy hoodies, quiet sneakers and accessories that look better after dark.

The brand is fictional. This website was built from scratch with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript as a learning project: a full shop with categories, product pages, a cart and a test checkout.

What is on this site

  • Categories for men, women, kids and sale
  • Search, filters and sorting
  • A page for every product, with sizes and photos
  • A cart that remembers your items
  • Checkout in Stripe test mode
