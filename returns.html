Shipping & returns

Shipping

Orders ship in 2 to 4 working days. Shipping is free on every order. You get a tracking link by email when your order leaves the warehouse.

Returns

You can return unused items in their original condition within 30 days of delivery, free of charge. Contact us first at our contact page and we will send you a return label.

Right of withdrawal (EU)

Customers in the EU have a legal right to cancel an online purchase within 14 days of receiving the goods, without giving a reason. Our 30-day policy is longer than this and includes it.

Refunds

When we have received your return, the money goes back to the card you paid with within 14 days.
